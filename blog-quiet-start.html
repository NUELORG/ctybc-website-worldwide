A Quiet Start
Building a daily rhythm of Scripture

A steady faith does not need a dramatic beginning. It can begin with a few quiet minutes, an open Bible and a question carried into the day.

CTYBC JOURNALREFLECTION & PRACTICAL GUIDANCE
A small, repeatable rhythm can help a young reader meet Scripture with curiosity rather than pressure. Choose a short passage. Read it slowly. Ask what is happening, what the passage says about God, and what one small response could look like today.

Start with a Gospel story or a Psalm. Keep a notebook for questions and words worth remembering. A parent, teacher or mentor can read alongside the young person, making space for conversation instead of turning every reading into a test. Some days the conversation may be brief. Returning tomorrow matters more than finishing a long checklist.

Put it into practice

Children grow at different paces, so make the rhythm flexible. A verse at breakfast, a story before bed or a short family reflection can all become ways to keep Scripture near. Proverbs 22:6 points toward patient formation over time.

The aim is not to know every answer at once. It is to become comfortable listening, asking and learning from the Word, one day at a time.
