CHAIRMAN

Asst. Pastor Dr. Unenwojo Sarah Edah

We are privileged to have Asst. Pastor Dr. Unenwojo Sarah Edah serve as Chairman of the Board of Trustees of Catch Them Young Bible Club (CTYBC). She is the Proprietress of The Golden Crest Royal Academy and holds an honorary doctorate degree in Child Psychology. Her spiritual depth, leadership experience and passion for godly child upbringing provide strong direction and stability for the vision and mission of CTYBC. We are deeply grateful for her guidance, wisdom and unwavering commitment to raising a generation firmly grounded in Christ.

TRUSTEE

Dr (Mrs) Temitayo Felicia KehindeDada

Dr (Mrs) Temitayo Felicia KehindeDada is a committed Christian and Deaconess with a deep passion for the spiritual growth and godly upbringing of children. She holds a PhD in Parliamentary Administration and serves in Nigeria’s National Assembly. She is the founder of Regale Foundation for the Less Privileged, a faith-based outreach dedicated to supporting vulnerable children and families through compassion, service and the love of Christ. Her heart for ministry is centered on mentoring young minds and raising children who fear God and walk in righteousness. As a member of the Board of Trustees, Dr KehindeDada brings prayerful leadership, commitment and a strong conviction that children grounded in God’s Word will grow to become responsible and godly leaders in their generation. Her insight, generosity and commitment to service contribute to ensuring CTYBC’s programmes remain impactful, ethical and truly child-centred.

OUR FOUNDATION

Vision & mission.

CTYBC is a non-profit children and youth outreach committed to Bible-based learning, mentorship and life skills development. The club organises school and community outreaches, Bible quiz competitions, capacity-building programmes and other activities to inspire and guide the next generation.

VISION

To see every child and teenager grow into a Christ-centered adult who influences society positively.

MISSION

To reach and nurture children and teenagers with the message of Christ, equipping them spiritually, mentally and socially to become godly leaders of tomorrow.
