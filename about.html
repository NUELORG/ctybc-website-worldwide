HOME/OUR STORY
CATCH THEM YOUNG BIBLE CLUB
JAWOM · ABUJA, NIGERIA
OUR PURPOSE

Plant faith early.
Equip young people for life.

The club’s work is centred on helping children and teenagers know the Bible, grow in character and become responsible leaders.

CTYBC gatherings include Bible stories and teaching, prayer, praise and worship, games and time with friends. The club’s public profile describes its mission as the thorough upbringing of godly children as great and respectable leaders.

Independent reporting in 2025 traced the club’s beginnings to Emmanuel Sani Ujah’s recovery after a serious accident in 2014. The first club gathering brought together six children.

VISIONARY & FOUNDER

Min Emmanuel
Sani Ujah

A conviction that young people deserve a strong foundation in faith became the beginning of CTYBC.

In a 2025 interview, Emmanuel Sani Ujah described how a serious accident in 2014 and a period of recovery changed the course of his life. After he recovered, he began gathering children to learn Scripture. The first group had six children, and the work grew into Catch Them Young Bible Club.

Today, the club’s approach brings Bible teaching together with prayer, mentorship, fellowship and practical learning. This portrait was provided by CTYBC for the founder’s profile.
