CTYBC invited children and teenagers to take part in its first Bible Quiz Competition in Abuja.

21 NOVEMBER 2026 · 10:00 AMFREE REGISTRATION
A Bible knowledge competition for adolescents and teenagers, with a first screening on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

CTYBC’s Facebook artwork lists two age groups: adolescents aged 10–13 and teenagers aged 14–16. Registration is free. Event materials advertise cash prizes over ₦300,000 plus additional gifts.

Venue and screening

The Golden Crest Royal Academy, Airport Road, Abuja. CTYBC materials vary between “ANAC Estate” and “AMAC Estate” and also name Angwan Sawa, FCT. Confirm directions with the club before travelling.
