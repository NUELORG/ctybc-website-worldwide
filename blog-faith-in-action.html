Faith Beyond Sunday
Turning care into practical service

Faith becomes visible in the way we treat people around us. For young people, service can begin close to home and grow through small acts of attention.

CTYBC JOURNALREFLECTION & PRACTICAL GUIDANCE
Ask a simple question: what does someone near me need today? It might be help carrying something, a thoughtful message to a classmate, sharing a skill or taking part in a community effort with trusted adults. Small actions teach responsibility when they are offered with respect.

Service should protect dignity. Adults can explain the purpose of a project, ask communities what support is useful, and make sure children are supervised and safe. Young people should understand that helping is not about being praised or photographed; it is about treating neighbours as people whose voices matter.

Put it into practice

Matthew 5:16 connects good works with a light that points beyond itself. Families and groups can reflect together after serving: What did we learn? What should we do differently next time? Who else should be part of the conversation?

When service is thoughtful and consistent, children learn that compassion is a practice. They can carry that habit into their homes, schools and wider communities.
